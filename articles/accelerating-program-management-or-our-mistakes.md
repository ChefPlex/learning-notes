# Are We Using AI to Accelerate Program Management, or Our Mistakes?

Are we using AI to accelerate program management, or are we just accelerating our mistakes?

Spent some time over the weekend doing a deep dive into current research and articles, and the industry data shows a sobering gap. In a survey Qlik commissioned in August 2025, 97% of more than 200 large-enterprise technology leaders had committed budget to agentic AI, but only 18% had fully deployed it. MIT NANDA's preliminary 2025 study (52 interviews, 153 survey responses, 300+ public initiatives) found that only about 5% of integrated GenAI pilots produced marked, sustained value, while the rest showed no measurable P&L impact within about six months. The study names the cause as integration with real workflows, not model quality. Both are early and imperfect numbers, but they point the same way: the bottleneck isn't model capability, it is workflow integration and a lack of structured governance. True competitive advantage in a modern PMO does not come from simply deploying more AI, but from governing it with precision.

From tracking a critical new competency like AI cost governance to preventing runaway cloud bills from ungoverned automation, the Technical Program Manager's role is transitioning from execution tracking to strategic engineering.

The most dangerous pitfall for program leaders today is the "Hallucinating Progress" trap. Because generative AI can draft flawless, highly professional status reports in seconds, project dashboards can easily show a sea of healthy green. But without vigilant human-in-the-loop oversight, these beautiful reports can hide underlying structural decay, driven by our own automation bias.

As program leaders, we must design rigorous evaluation criteria, understand the distinct risk profiles of RAG and Agentic systems, and remember that while AI can tell us *what* is happening, it remains our job as human leaders to interpret the *why*.

OK, 3 quick takeaways:

1) **Bandwidth Reclamation:** By automating routine administrative tasks, AI reclaims time for Technical Program Managers (TPMs) to focus on high-impact, strategic execution. Measure the time saved on your own program before you promise it to anyone.

2) **Solving the Integration Bottleneck:** Because most corporate AI pilots stall on workflow integration rather than model capability, TPMs are uniquely positioned to step up as the leaders who design and govern these new AI workflows.

3) **Elevating Human Leadership to the "Why":** Delegating routine data aggregation to AI elevates the TPM's role from a tactical coordinator to a strategic leader who interprets the "why" behind program trends, builds cross-functional trust, and executes critical human-in-the-loop decisions.
